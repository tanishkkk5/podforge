# AI helper — Creator Caption Writer

- **One job:** write LinkedIn and Instagram captions for chosen clips, in a voice that fits one creator's niche.
- **Never:** invents facts beyond the clip; the credit line and episode link are added by code, not by the AI.
- **Instructions live in:** `lib/creatorCaptions.ts`
- **Owner:** Tanisk Pandey
- **Human check:** creator kits start as Draft and must be approved before the creator's link works.
- **Measure:** note how often captions are rewritten before approval.
