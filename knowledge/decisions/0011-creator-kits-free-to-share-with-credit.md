# 0011 — Creator kit clips are free to share, with credit

**Decided:** 2026-10 · **Gates tripped:** touches a commitment (a promise to outside creators)

**Context:** Creator Kits send niche-matched clips and captions to other content creators so they can post them.

**Decision:** clips in a creator kit may be posted on the creator's own channels free of charge, as long as the credit line and episode link stay in. We ask (not require) that they tag Luke on LinkedIn or use an Instagram Collab post.

**Alternatives:** paid sponsorships; no sharing rights (ask case by case).

**Rationale:** extra reach for the show and guests at no cost; one clear rule is easier for creators than negotiating each time.

**Consequences:** the credit line is added by code so it can't be dropped. If we ever pay a creator, the post must be labeled as sponsored (US FTC rule) — that would need a new decision. Guests should be comfortable with their clips being shared this way.

**Status:** accepted
