# 0015 — Saturday launches, 24-hour share window, affiliate rules

**Decided:** 2026-10 · **Gates tripped:** reverses an earlier decision (0006), sets a convention, touches a commitment

**Context:** episodes earn through the Amazon affiliate tag (`profitstrea0b-20`) on book links. Reach — and so
affiliate clicks — depends most on posts in the first 24 hours after launch. Amazon's Associates rules restrict
where affiliate links may appear.

**Decision:**
1. Episodes go live **Saturdays at 7:30 AM ET** (Apple Podcasts + Spotify, MP3 audio). Replaces Sunday (0006).
2. Guests are told up front (intake form) and again on the **Friday before launch** (kit email) that the first
   24 hours matter most. The intake form has an **optional** "I'll share within 24 hours" tick-box — never required.
3. **Guests never receive or post AF's Amazon affiliate link.** Their captions link to the **episode**; the episode
   notes carry the book links. (Amazon only allows Special Links on sites listed in AF's Associates account.)
4. **AF's own LinkedIn page is registered** with Amazon Associates → every AF LinkedIn post carries the tagged book
   link + disclosure (in a "first comment", which LinkedIn tends to show more widely than links in the post body).
5. **AF's Instagram is NOT registered** → never an Amazon link on Instagram.
6. **No Amazon links in emails to guests** (Amazon restricts Special Links in email); emails link to the kit/episode.
7. Every place with an Amazon link shows: *"As an Amazon Associate, Applied Frameworks earns from qualifying
   purchases."* (show notes) or *"#ad · As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases."* (LinkedIn).
8. Calls to action are plain ("📘 Get the book") — no rewards, no "buy through our link to support us", and never
   ask guests or colleagues to buy through AF's link.
9. If a guest hasn't posted **24 hours after launch**, a friendly reminder is drafted (was: 7 days after the email).
10. **Where Amazon links live** (confirmed Oct 2026: registered sites are **profit-streams.com** and **AF's LinkedIn**):
    - each episode's **page on profit-streams.com** carries the tagged book links + disclosure ("Website" show notes);
    - **Spotify and Apple descriptions are not registered** → their show notes ("Spotify / Apple" version) contain
      **no Amazon links**; every book points to the episode page instead;
    - **guests' posts link to the episode page** first (then Spotify/Apple if no page yet) — so guest traffic lands
      where the affiliate links are.

**Alternatives:** ask guests to post the affiliate link directly (rejected — likely breaks the Associates rules);
Sunday launches (0006).

**Rationale:** concentrate reach in the launch window while keeping the Associates account safe.

**Consequences:** the guest kit email is sent on Fridays; marking an episode **Published** records the launch time
that starts the 24h window. Registering Instagram (and Spotify/Apple, if Amazon allows) would let those carry book links — a later decision.

**Status:** accepted
