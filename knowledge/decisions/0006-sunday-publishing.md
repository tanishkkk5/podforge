# 0006 — Publish every Sunday at 7:30 AM ET

**Decided:** 2026-09 · **Gates tripped:** sets a convention, changes how people work

**Context:** episodes went out on no fixed day, so promotion was ad hoc.

**Decision:** release every episode on Sunday at 7:30 AM ET (5:00 PM IST).

**Alternatives:** weekday releases; no fixed schedule.

**Rationale:** matches Lenny's Podcast's consistent Sunday releases (checked against real episode dates), and 7:30 AM ET lines up with the start of the producer's working day in India, so promotion starts right after release.

**Consequences:** episodes must be edited and scheduled before the weekend; social posts go out Sunday evening IST. Exceptions (e.g. a Saturday release) should be noted in the episode's tracker.

**Status:** superseded by 0015 (Saturday 7:30 AM ET launches, Oct 2026)
