# 0007 — Lenny-format show notes

**Decided:** 2026-09 · **Gates tripped:** sets a convention, says "no" (to three sections)

**Context:** show notes varied episode to episode and missed resources and related episodes.

**Decision:** use the Lenny's Podcast show-notes structure (see the standard) for every episode.

**Alternatives:** keep the old short description; build our own format from scratch.

**Rationale:** a proven, listener-friendly structure; complete resources help guests and SEO.

**Consequences:** three Lenny sections are deliberately excluded — transcript archive links (not built), sponsor section (business decision), named recurring segment (Luke's open call).

**Status:** accepted
