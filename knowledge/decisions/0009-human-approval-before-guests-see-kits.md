# 0009 — Human approval before guests see kits

**Decided:** 2026-10 · **Gates tripped:** changes how people work, touches a commitment (what we send guests)

**Context:** guest and creator kit links worked the moment they were generated, so AI-written captions could reach people with no human check. The AI-native workshop stressed: own your output and review it before anyone else has to.

**Decision:** kits start as Draft. Public links show "being prepared" until someone marks the kit Approved (name and date recorded). Regenerating resets to Draft. All existing kits were reset to Draft for review.

**Alternatives:** keep links live immediately; a manual checklist with no enforcement.

**Rationale:** one bad caption or wrong name sent to a guest costs more trust than a short review costs time.

**Consequences:** sending a kit now takes one extra click after reading it. Previously shared links show "being prepared" until re-approved.

**Status:** accepted
