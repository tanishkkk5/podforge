# Guest outreach message

Send from Luke's LinkedIn (or as an email **draft** for Luke). Personalize the middle line with something specific to the person — their book, research, or a recent post. Personalized hooks are expected to land better than the generic version (the first 10 generic messages got no replies).

> Hi [Name] — I'm Luke Hohmann, host of the Profit Streams® Podcast at Applied Frameworks. We've had some great conversations lately with folks like Nir Eyal and Mik Kersten, and I think you'd bring a perspective our audience of product, pricing, and business leaders would really value.
>
> [One specific line about them — e.g. "Your 2026 monetization survey is exactly the kind of evidence our listeners ask about."]
>
> Would you be up for coming on the show for a conversation? Happy to work around your schedule — always remote, always low-lift.

**Don't** promise a specific time length (e.g. "just 30 minutes") — prep and follow-up make the real commitment longer.
