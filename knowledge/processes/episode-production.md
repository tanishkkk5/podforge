# Episode production — from booking to promotion

**Start each day in Podforge → ✨ Production Assistant.** It tracks every episode through
Intake → Booked → Recorded → Kit drafted → Kit approved → Published, lists what needs you, and drafts
follow-ups and prep briefs for you to approve (decision 0013).

1. **Book the guest** → see `guest-scheduling.md`. Guest fills in the intake form (`/`).
2. **Record** on Riverside (decision 0008).
3. **Edit** the episode and cut 3 Magic Clips. Use **Clip Finder** with Riverside's `.srt` export to find the best moments first.
4. **Drive folder**: create `Profit Streams Podcast (Host & Guest)/` in the podcast root folder with
   `Main Podcast/` (title, summary, takeaways, chapters, show notes, transcript, resources, bio, thumbnail, video)
   and `Magic Clips/` (3 clips + their transcripts). Share each video file as "Anyone with the link can view".
5. **Drop Riverside's `.srt` export into the Drive drop folder** (decision 0014). Production Assistant notices it.
6. **Episode Studio** (`/admin/production`, decision 0016): pick the guest from their intake form (host, photo,
   title and links fill in; episode # = last guest kit + 1), paste the Riverside transcript + chapters (or ask
   Claude in Cowork to pull them from Riverside), click **Check names**, then **Draft episode + guest kit**.
   (The Episode Pipeline with the Drive drop folder still works for chapters + clip ideas.)
7. **Shape it** on the episode page, top to bottom: **Title** (✨ 5 ideas, edit) → **Episode #** → **Description**
   (hook, summary, 10 takeaways, quote, chapters; ✨ refresh any of them) → **Save + rebuild images** →
   **Resources** (paste real links, never guess) → **Show notes** (Spotify/Apple + Website versions).
   Open the **guest kit preview** to edit or ✨ refresh each caption and the hashtags.
8. **Review and approve** (review standard). Approved = final and locked. Then **Build / update guest Drive
   folder**, drop the audio, the 3 Magic Clips and the Canva thumbnail into their folders, and send the guest
   their kit link + Drive link (thumbnail attached in the email for now).
8b. **After publishing:** paste Spotify, Apple, the episode page and Luke's LinkedIn post into "Links after
   publishing" and Save. Every caption, the Download all zip and (after re-running it) the Drive bundle use them.
9. **Add to the Content Library**: the episode + its 3 clips, with Drive video links. Check the AI's topic tags.
10. **Friday:** approve and send the guest their kit email ("goes live tomorrow" — no Amazon links).
    **Saturday 7:30 AM ET: publish** (decision 0015), then mark the episode **Published** in Production Assistant
    (this records the launch time for the 24-hour share window).
11. **Promote**: approve the Social Pack (LinkedIn + Instagram posts) and the guest's share email; mark
    "guest posted" when they do (a reminder is drafted 24 hours after launch if not). AF LinkedIn posts carry the
    book link + disclosure in the first comment; Instagram never gets an Amazon link. Make Creator Kits — approve before sending.
12. **Monday:** weekly stats check-in (`weekly-stats.md`).

Done when: episode live · guest has an approved kit · show notes published · clips in the library.
