# Start here

**What this is:** Podforge is the tool behind the Profit Streams® Podcast — guest intake and scheduling,
guest kits and show notes, a topic-tagged library of every episode and clip, and kits for other creators.

**Read in this order (about 20 minutes):**
1. `CLAUDE.md` — the always-on rules (one page).
2. `knowledge/processes/episode-production.md` — how an episode goes from booking to promotion.
3. `knowledge/standards/` — brand spelling, show-notes format, links, review.
4. `knowledge/decisions/` — why things are the way they are.
5. `knowledge/domains/` — our frameworks, brand visuals and approved messaging.

**Your first week:**
- Get the shared admin password from the owner and log in at `/admin/login`.
- Open **Guest Kits & Show Notes** and read one approved kit end to end.
- Open the **Content Library**, filter by a topic, and watch one clip.
- Shadow one episode through the production process before running one yourself.

**Who to ask:** Tanisk Pandey (owner of Podforge and the podcast knowledge base) — tpandey@appliedframeworks.com.
Podcast host: Luke Hohmann.

**Golden rules:** AI drafts, a person approves · never invent a link · always "Profit Streams®".
